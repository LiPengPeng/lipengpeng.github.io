隐私政策

跑车过收费站 · 最后更新 2026-10-20

本政策说明「跑车过收费站」（下称「本 App」）收集哪些信息、为什么收集、交给谁处理，以及你可以怎么关掉。

1. 我们收集什么

  • 基础设备信息与使用统计：统计选择开启后，Firebase Analytics 会自动处理应用实例标识、启动/会话、设备与系统信息及粗略地区；本 App 不发送自定义的过站/游戏进度事件。
  • 广告相关信息：Google Mobile Ads 可能处理设备标识、粗略地区、广告展示与互动、性能及诊断信息，用于广告投放、频次限制、效果衡量与安全。

我们不收集你的姓名、联系方式、精确位置，也不会收集你在 App 内输入的任何自由文本——本 App 没有输入框。

2. 用来做什么

  • 在你允许统计后，了解基础使用情况并改进游戏体验；
  • 展示广告、管理广告频次并衡量广告效果；
  • 支持广告服务的安全运行与必要诊断。

3. 交给谁处理

统计与广告由 Google 提供：

  • Firebase Analytics（Google Analytics for Firebase）——使用统计
  • Google AdMob——广告投放
  • Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）——广告同意管理

相关说明见 Google 隐私权政策 与 Google 如何使用合作网站/应用的信息。

4. 什么时候开始收集

首次启动会显示「隐私与统计选择」弹框。只有你选择「允许使用统计」后，Firebase Analytics 才开启；选择「不允许，继续游戏」时统计保持关闭，游戏功能不受影响。广告隐私选择由 Google UMP 按所在地区和后台配置单独处理。

拒绝个性化广告不代表停止所有广告相关数据处理；非个性化广告仍可能使用设备标识进行频次控制和汇总衡量。

5. 怎么关掉

App 内：设置 → 使用统计，关掉后立即停止后续收集。撤回只影响之后的收集，不影响撤回前已经产生的统计。

如 Google UMP 要求提供广告隐私入口，App 设置中会显示广告隐私选项，可打开 Google 的隐私表单更改相关选择。

系统级：iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」可以关闭跨 App 跟踪。

6. 保存与删除

Firebase Analytics 的事件级数据保留期由对应 Google Analytics 属性的设置决定；广告数据由 Google 按其服务政策与配置处理。本 App 不运营用户账号或自有服务器数据库；最佳纪录仅保存在本机，卸载 App 后删除。你可通过 App Store 产品页的开发者联系方式提出数据访问、更正或删除请求。

7. 儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会主动收集儿童的个人信息。

8. 你的权利

你可以要求了解、更正或删除与你相关的数据。由于本 App 不设用户账号，无法仅凭账号识别 Google 处理的数据；请通过 App Store 产品页的开发者联系方式提交请求，我们会协助处理或转交相关服务提供方。

9. 变更

如果收集范围、服务提供方或数据用途发生实质变化，我们会在 App 内重新征求同意，并更新本页面的「最后更新」日期。

Privacy Policy

Toll Booth Roadster · Last updated 2026-10-20

This policy explains what Toll Booth Roadster (the “App”) collects, why, who processes it, and how you can turn it off.

1. What we collect

  • Device information and usage statistics: after analytics is enabled, Firebase Analytics automatically processes an app-instance identifier, launches and sessions, device and OS information, and coarse region. This app does not send custom booth or gameplay-progress events.
  • Ad-related data: Google Mobile Ads may process device identifiers, coarse region, ad impressions and interactions, performance, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, and security.

We do not collect your name, contact details, precise location, or any free text — the App has no text input.

2. Why

  • Understand basic usage and improve the game after you allow analytics;
  • Display ads, manage frequency and measure ad performance;
  • Support the security and diagnostics needed to operate the ad service.

3. Who processes it

Analytics and ads are provided by Google:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) — usage statistics
  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — ad consent management

See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites and apps.

4. When collection starts

On first launch the App shows a “Privacy & Analytics” dialog. Firebase Analytics starts only after you choose Allow analytics. If you choose “Don't allow, continue playing”, analytics stay off and every game feature still works. Google UMP handles ad-related privacy choices separately according to your region and the configured messages.

Declining personalised ads does not stop all ad-related processing; non-personalised ads may still use device identifiers for frequency capping and aggregated measurement.

5. How to turn it off

In the App: Settings → Usage analytics. Turning it off stops further collection immediately; it does not affect data already collected.

If Google UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, Settings shows Ad privacy options, which opens Google's privacy form.

At system level: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking lets you disable cross-app tracking.

6. Retention and deletion

Firebase Analytics event-level retention is controlled by the corresponding Google Analytics property's settings. Google processes ad data under its service policies and configuration. The App has no user accounts or server database of its own; your best score is stored on device and deleted when you uninstall the App. You can request access, correction, or deletion through the developer contact on the App Store product page.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of data relating to you. Because the App has no user accounts, it cannot identify Google-held data by account; submit a request through the developer contact on the App Store product page and we will help process or forward it to the relevant service provider.

9. Changes

If the scope of collection, processors or purposes change materially, we will ask for consent again inside the App and update the date on this page.

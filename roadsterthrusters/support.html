常见问题

怎么玩？

上下滑动在三条车道间换道。ETC 通行券有限，消耗 1 张快速通过得 2 分；人工车道得 1 分；服务区可以补券。关闭车道会撞上栏杆。看清每个收费站的标牌，提前决定路线。

车道状态会变化吗？

会。每站会重新安排 ETC 快速、人工、服务区和关闭路线中的三种状态。ETC 通行券最多保存 3 张，用完后无法通过 ETC 车道；留意标牌并在栏杆前完成换道。

声音没了？

检查 App 内「设置 → 音效」是否被关掉，以及手机是否处于静音模式（本 App 的音效会跟随系统静音开关）。

怎么关掉统计？

App 内「设置 → 使用统计」关掉即可，不影响游戏。详见隐私政策。

为什么会有广告？

广告用于支持后续开发。目前只在结算后展示，每三局最多一次。广告隐私选择由 Google UMP 管理；如需要更改，打开 App「设置 → 广告隐私选项」。

进度会丢吗？

最佳纪录保存在本机。删除 App 会一并删除。

FAQ

How do I play?

Swipe up or down to switch between three lanes. ETC passes are limited: spend one for an express crossing worth 2 points. The manual lane earns 1, and service stops refill a pass. A closed lane ends the run. Read each plaza sign before reaching the barrier.

Do lane conditions change?

Yes. Each plaza places three states from the ETC express lane, manual lane, service stop, and closed lane. You can keep up to three ETC passes; without one, the express lane is unavailable. Check the signs before reaching the barrier.

No sound?

Check Settings → Sound in the app, and your device’s silent switch (the game follows it).

How do I turn off analytics?

Settings → Usage analytics in the app. Gameplay is unaffected. See the Privacy Policy.

Why ads?

Ads support further development. They only appear after a run ends, at most once every three runs. Google UMP manages ad privacy choices; when the option is required, open Settings → Ad privacy options in the App.

Will I lose my progress?

Your best score is stored on device. Deleting the app deletes it.

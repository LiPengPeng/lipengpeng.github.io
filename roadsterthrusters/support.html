常见问题

怎么玩？

来车到达分流点后，点选 ETC、人工或服务区按钮，在倒计时结束前安排它的车道。车辆需求与车道匹配可得更多分；每条队列最多 4 辆，超时车辆会自动改派。累计三次调度失误结束当班。

各条路线有什么区别？

ETC 处理较快，但每派一辆车消耗一张通行券；人工车道通用稳定；服务区处理较慢，匹配服务需求后补回一张券。开局有两张，最多保存三张。

声音没了？

检查 App 内「设置 → 音效」是否被关掉，以及手机是否处于静音模式（本 App 的音效会跟随系统静音开关）。

怎么关掉统计？

App 内「设置 → 使用统计」关掉即可，不影响游戏。详见隐私政策。

为什么会有广告？

广告用于支持后续开发。目前只在结算后展示，每三局最多一次。广告隐私选择由 Google UMP 管理；如需要更改，打开 App「设置 → 广告隐私选项」。

进度会丢吗？

最佳纪录保存在本机。删除 App 会一并删除。

FAQ

How do I play?

When a car reaches the dispatch line, tap ETC, cash, or service before the timer expires. Matching its request earns more points. Each queue holds four cars; timed-out cars are rerouted. Three dispatch mistakes end the shift.

What is different about each lane?

ETC processes cars quickly but uses one pass per car. Cash is a steady all-purpose lane. Service is slower and earns a pass when it handles a matching break request. You start with two passes and can hold up to three.

No sound?

Check Settings → Sound in the app, and your device’s silent switch (the game follows it).

How do I turn off analytics?

Settings → Usage analytics in the app. Gameplay is unaffected. See the Privacy Policy.

Why ads?

Ads support further development. They only appear after a run ends, at most once every three runs. Google UMP manages ad privacy choices; when the option is required, open Settings → Ad privacy options in the App.

Will I lose my progress?

Your best score is stored on device. Deleting the app deletes it.

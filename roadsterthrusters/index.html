Toll Booth Roadster

看清目标车速，精准通过收费站

驶向收费杆时，先滑动选定车道，再按住左侧刹车或右侧油门，将车速调到标牌目标。每站的车道顺序和目标速度都会变化；连续三次速度失误会结束行程。

ETC 车道要求通行券，人工车道无需通行券，服务区可以补充一张。距离目标越近，得到的路线分越多。

免费、离线可玩、无需登录，无内购。

Match the target speed at every toll booth

Swipe to select a lane, then hold Brake or Accelerate to match the speed shown on its sign before reaching the barrier. Lane order and speed targets change at each plaza. Three missed speed checks end the trip.

ETC uses a pass, manual lanes need none, and service stops refill one. The closer you get to the target, the more route points you earn.

Free to play offline. No sign-in or in-app purchases.

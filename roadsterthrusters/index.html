Toll Booth Dispatch

读懂来车需求，平衡收费队列

在分流倒计时结束前，把车辆派到 ETC、人工或服务区。留意每条队列的容量和处理时间，避免车流积压。超时车辆会自动改派并计入失误。

ETC 放行快但消耗通行券；人工车道通用稳定；服务区处理较慢，匹配服务需求后可以补回通行券。

免费、离线可玩、无需登录，无内购。

Route cars and balance three toll queues

Read each car's request and assign it to ETC, cash, or service before the dispatch timer expires. Watch lane capacity and processing times to keep traffic moving. Timed-out cars are rerouted and count as a mistake.

ETC clears quickly but uses a pass. Cash is flexible, while service is slower and rewards a matching request with a pass.

Free to play offline. No sign-in or in-app purchases.
